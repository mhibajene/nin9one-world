## Product Posture

NIN9ONE Universe is an interactive spatial cultural experience built using real-time 3D technology. The term is a creative/product umbrella and confirms no additional fictional worlds. Technical game-development techniques may help deliver the experience; quests, objectives, inventories, progression, collectible loops, minimaps, persistent HUDs, NPC systems, economies, reward mechanics, and level-completion logic are not default requirements.

Bridge experience intent → spatial composition → technical implementation. Use place, artifact, state, ritual, and residue as product primitives without turning every primitive into a new system. Preserve standalone value for someone unfamiliar with the music and lore. Music can be gravitational without setting the product boundary; music and world changes have distinct cadences.

Progressive revelation favors depth, environmental change, reinterpretation, authored encounters, implied inaccessible space, and unresolved questions over geographic or content volume. Each currently accessible state must still offer a coherent first visit. Distinguish productive mystery from inability to orient, control, perceive entry, or use essential access functions.

## Spatial Reasoning

For an authorized task, begin with the observed experience and one concrete visitor-facing intention. Identify the existing spatial relationship that fails, then choose the smallest reversible expression that tests that intention. Do not prefill a three-move window from this guidance.

| Design concern | NIN9ONE-specific decision |
| --- | --- |
| Composition, blocking and sightlines | Keep the Citadel as the exterior's dominant destination. Judge the relationship between architecture, solar field, Black Water, foreground and horizon through movement, not just one still. Use existing masses and paths before adding competing anchors. |
| Scale and traversal | Use changing foreground relationships, parallax, approach distance, relative mass and atmosphere to communicate movement through somewhere. More camera freedom or greater zoom does not inherently improve traversal. Preserve control and responsive-device framing. |
| Occlusion and reveal | Let a partial view, framing edge or material trace create anticipation without implying unauthorized world facts. Inaccessible space can deepen curiosity; a visually promised route that only produces control confusion needs examination. |
| Threshold choreography | Exterior Threshold Legibility concerns a plausible entry and anticipation. Threshold Transformation concerns a separately authorized crossing that changes the experience through space, acoustics, light, visibility, movement or material response. Never merge these two gates. |
| Compression, expansion and interior atmosphere | Once interior work is authorized, make contrast serve the authored encounter and the visitor's attention. Start with one threshold and one encounter, not a complete interior plan. A proposed chamber remains a hypothesis until dispositioned. |
| Material and lighting hierarchy | Preserve darkness, intentional gold, atmospheric scale, silhouette and reflection. Presentation tokens do not establish substances. Do not promote emerging Glass or exploratory Living Filament through asset or shader choices. |
| Sound-space relationships | Let opt-in sound support perceived place, distance and enclosure where authorized. Preserve silence and reversible control. No unreleased audio, media-player pipeline or distribution infrastructure follows from spatial sound reasoning. |
| Authored-object placement | Decide what attention or interpretation depends on this particular object, its sightline and its spatial context. Meaning should survive without explanatory labels, item collection or reward UI. |
| Runtime construction | Reuse modular geometry, data-driven entities and atmospheric systems. Compare cost and perceptual value on intended devices; select geometry density, transparency, lights and asset size proportionally. Avoid speculative registries, dependencies or pipelines. |

Trophy Room is a preferred non-canonical product hypothesis. Throne Room meaning, function and occupant remain unresolved. Neither is authorized by this skill. Canon's physical and symbolic Citadel roles may guide interpretation without being explained to visitors or embodied as ranks or levels.

## Assets for Specificity

Use assets for specificity, not volume. Procedural construction remains appropriate for much architecture, atmosphere and runtime behavior. Consider an authored asset when a hero artifact, ceremonial form, bespoke threshold element, sculpture, architectural anchor, irregular natural form or cultural object depends on a particular shape or material expression.

Before proposing an asset, identify the perceptual purpose, relevant canon/material classification, intended placement and device cost. Treat external libraries as source material: assess fit, provenance and permitted use when acquisition is actually in scope. An attractive asset does not enter the world merely because it fills space. References do not become canon. Do not acquire assets, introduce dependencies, or adopt Blender as a production pipeline merely because this skill mentions them.

## Reviewable Output

Keep output proportional: one visitor-facing intention, the supporting observation, a spatial explanation or small diagram if useful, the bounded technical approach, relevant authority limits, and the evidence that will distinguish success from failure.

When implementation is authorized, inspect rendered approach, rest, movement extremes and relevant transitions on intended devices. Use technical checks to verify stability and visitor evidence to assess experience; neither alone declares the landscape complete. State separately whether the work preserves confirmed meaning (canon integrity) and advances the adopted experience without dilution or excess scope (product integrity).
