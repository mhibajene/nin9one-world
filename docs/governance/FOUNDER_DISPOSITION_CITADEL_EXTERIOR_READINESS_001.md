# Founder Disposition — Citadel Exterior Readiness & Spatial Experience Direction

**Status:** Founder-approved strategic disposition

**Recorded:** 2026-09-05, from the Founder's supplied disposition

**Authority class:** Product / experience governance

**Canon effect:** None

**Implementation authority:** Limited to the explicitly authorized next moves below

**Supersedes:** No canon, material-language, or implementation authority

## 1. Context

Citadel Discovery Window 007 is closed at 3/3.

The adopted `CITADEL_EXPERIENCE_VISION.md` establishes the Citadel as the first proof that the NIN9ONE Universe can function as a cultural experience with a life of its own rather than as a subordinate promotional surface for music.

The completed exterior work is accepted as a strong foundation.

The Landscape Completion Gate is **not yet met**.

Codex proposed the following progression:

1. Define an observable Landscape Completion Gate Protocol.
2. Conduct bounded observation with people uninvolved in building the Citadel.
3. Use the evidence to determine whether the exterior requires a bounded correction or is ready for a separate threshold/interior disposition.

Hegel independently reviewed the proposed gate and issued **CHALLENGE — Founder attention recommended**.

The challenge is accepted.

## 2. Founder Disposition

## ACCEPT CHALLENGE → RECONCILE GATE → OBSERVE → DISPOSE

First-contact visitor behaviour remains the correct primary evidence method for determining whether the exterior experience is complete.

However:

> **The six visitor-facing dimensions are necessary but not independently sufficient to declare the exterior complete.**

The gate will therefore contain two layers.

## 3. Visitor-Facing Experience Spine

The first-contact observation should evaluate six primary experiential signals.

### Destination

Does the visitor naturally understand that the Citadel is a significant destination toward which the environment draws them?

### Journey

Does movement through the landscape feel like traversal through a place rather than manipulation of a camera around a scene?

### Scale

Does the Citadel communicate increasing monumentality, distance and physical presence through approach?

### Responsiveness

Does the environment meaningfully answer visitor attention or movement through the systems already established, including atmosphere, Black Water, sound, light, reflection or related behaviour?

Responsiveness must not be declared from the success of one isolated system.

### Threshold

For the exterior gate, threshold means:

> **The visitor perceives a plausible place of entry and feels invited or compelled to approach it.**

This is henceforth termed:

**Exterior Threshold Legibility**

It does **not** include the experience of crossing the threshold.

### Memory

Memory means what an uninvolved visitor retains after leaving the experience.

Useful evidence includes:

- a specific image;
- a particular location;
- an environmental event;
- a feeling associated with a place;
- an unresolved question;
- a desire to know what lies somewhere inaccessible.

Memory at this stage does **not** mean repeat visitation, long-term retention, world residue or return cadence.

## 4. Exterior Readiness Rails

The six dimensions above must be considered alongside the following readiness conditions.

### Surrounding Environment Distinction

The landscape must contain enough spatial and environmental distinction that the Citadel is experienced as occupying a world rather than existing inside an attractive technical tableau.

The visitor need not exhaustively understand the environment.

The environment must nevertheless feel like somewhere.

### Place Versus Demo

The experience should read primarily as an explorable place rather than:

- a Three.js showcase;
- a camera demonstration;
- a static cinematic environment;
- a promotional microsite.

Technical novelty is not sufficient evidence of completion.

### Standalone Value

The experience should provide curiosity, atmosphere or meaning to a visitor with no prior knowledge of:

- NIN9ONE music;
- Nexus IV;
- Solari mythology;
- the Traveler / Seeker / Sovereign transformation;
- the material ontology.

The world may be shaped by canon without requiring canon explanation.

`WORLD_CANON.md` remains the sole authority for confirmed lore, including the Citadel's role as both a physical destination and a symbolic place of realization and understanding.

### Intended-Device Experience

Performance, navigation and essential controls must be sufficient on the intended devices and must not materially undermine immersion.

The gate cannot pass purely on high-end desktop presentation if the intended audience will materially encounter the experience elsewhere.

Accessibility should enable access without requiring the experience to become interface-heavy.

## 5. Threshold Boundary

The following distinction is now explicit.

### Exterior Threshold Legibility

Belongs to the Landscape Completion Gate.

Question:

> Can the visitor perceive somewhere to enter, and does approaching it create anticipation?

### Threshold Transformation

Belongs to a future first-interior proof.

Question:

> Does crossing into the Citadel unmistakably change the nature of the experience?

Threshold Transformation may eventually involve:

- atmosphere;
- acoustics;
- spatial compression or expansion;
- material behaviour;
- lighting;
- movement;
- visibility;
- environmental response.

No such implementation is authorized by this disposition.

## 6. Exterior Completion Decision

Following first-contact observation, the Founder disposition must resolve to one of three outcomes.

### PASS

The exterior is considered experience-complete.

This closes the exterior proof.

A PASS does **not** automatically authorize interior implementation.

It permits a separate Founder consideration of:

> **Threshold + First Authored Encounter**

### PASS WITH ONE BOUNDED CORRECTION

Observation reveals one sufficiently repeated deficiency preventing closure.

One targeted correction may be authorized.

This should not become a general exterior polish window.

The relevant gate condition should then be reconsidered.

### NOT YET

Evidence indicates the exterior still fails one or more fundamental experience or readiness conditions.

Interior work remains closed.

Observed failures become evidence for a separately authorized exterior window.

## 7. First-Contact Observation Posture

Participants should be uninvolved in building the Citadel.

Where practical, observation should include variation across:

- NIN9ONE familiarity;
- music familiarity;
- design / interactive-culture familiarity;
- people with no contextual knowledge of the project;
- intended device classes.

Participants should receive minimal framing.

The purpose is to observe what the environment communicates without explanation.

Feedback is evidence, **not creative authority**.

Participant requests for conventional game or interface features should not be interpreted as implementation requirements.

The Founder retains product and creative judgment.

## 8. Progressive Revelation

The following product direction is accepted for future Citadel development:

> **The Citadel should reveal itself over time rather than present itself as a complete environment.**

This is a product and experience principle, not new lore.

Future expansion should generally prefer:

- meaningful revelation over content volume;
- depth within existing places over constant geographic expansion;
- inaccessible implied space where appropriate;
- environmental change;
- reinterpretation of previously encountered material;
- authored encounters;
- unresolved questions.

Mystery should not automatically be treated as incomplete content.

A first-time visitor should still be able to experience the current world coherently without knowledge of previous states.

The canon's existing treatment of journey, realization, gold, rupture and transformation provides thematic compatibility with this direction without requiring those concepts to be explicitly explained to visitors.

## 9. First Interior Principle

If and when the exterior gate passes, the Citadel should **not** immediately enter broad interior development.

The preferred first proof remains:

> **One threshold + one authored encounter.**

The objective is not to build “the interior of the Citadel.”

The objective is to determine whether crossing into the Citadel can reveal one culturally meaningful experience that:

- feels native to the universe;
- materially differs from exterior exploration;
- deepens curiosity;
- rewards attention;
- implies that more exists than is presently accessible.

The preferred Trophy Room direction remains a **non-canonical product hypothesis**.

The Throne Room's precise meaning, function and occupant remain unresolved.

No chamber is authorized by this disposition.

## 10. NIN9ONE_SPATIAL_EXPERIENCE

The creation of a repo-native spatial experience skill is **approved in principle**.

Working name:

`NIN9ONE_SPATIAL_EXPERIENCE`

### Purpose

The skill should provide Codex with project-specific guidance for reasoning about three-dimensional spatial experience without importing conventional game-development assumptions into the NIN9ONE Universe.

It should bridge:

**experience intent → spatial composition → technical implementation**

without becoming canon or implementation authority.

### Intended Scope

The skill may govern reasoning around:

- spatial composition;
- architectural blocking;
- sightlines;
- scale;
- traversal;
- threshold choreography;
- occlusion and reveal;
- spatial compression and expansion;
- environmental storytelling;
- lighting hierarchy;
- sound-space relationships;
- camera behaviour;
- interior atmosphere;
- placement of authored objects;
- implication of inaccessible space;
- performance-aware spatial construction;
- integration of procedural geometry and authored assets.

### Core Posture

The skill must treat NIN9ONE Universe primarily as:

> **an interactive spatial cultural experience built using real-time 3D technology**

rather than a conventional game.

It should explicitly resist default assumptions around:

- quests;
- objectives;
- inventories;
- progression systems;
- collectible loops;
- minimaps;
- HUDs;
- NPC systems;
- game economies;
- reward mechanics;
- level-completion logic.

Game-development techniques may be used where technically useful.

Game-design conventions are not automatically product requirements.

### Authority

The skill must inherit the following authority order:

1. Founder dispositions
2. `WORLD_CANON.md` for confirmed lore
3. adopted experience vision
4. approved material language
5. active work-window scope
6. spatial-experience guidance

The skill may not invent lore to solve spatial-design problems.

Architecture must not silently create canon.

## 11. Asset Posture

The future spatial skill should also encode the following principle:

> **Use assets for specificity, not volume.**

Procedural systems should continue to establish much of the world's architecture, atmosphere and runtime behaviour.

Authored assets become appropriate when meaning depends on specificity, including:

- hero artifacts;
- ceremonial forms;
- bespoke threshold elements;
- sculptural objects;
- distinctive architectural anchors;
- irregular natural forms;
- future culturally meaningful objects.

External asset libraries should be treated as source material rather than automatic world-ready content.

No external asset enters the NIN9ONE World solely because it fills visual space.

The canonical material ontology remains controlling where relevant.

## 12. Authorized Next Three Moves

### Move 1 — Landscape Completion Gate Protocol

Codex may define the observation protocol using:

**Visitor spine**

- Destination
- Journey
- Scale
- Responsiveness
- Exterior Threshold Legibility
- Memory

**Readiness rails**

- Surrounding-environment distinction
- Place-versus-demo
- Standalone value
- Intended-device performance and essential controls

Keep the protocol lightweight and observational.

Avoid excessive scoring infrastructure.

### Move 2 — Uninvolved First-Contact Observation

Conduct bounded observation against the approved protocol.

Do not redesign during observation.

Capture evidence before interpretation.

### Move 3 — Founder Exterior Disposition

Synthesize the evidence for Founder review.

Recommend only:

- PASS;
- PASS WITH ONE BOUNDED CORRECTION; or
- NOT YET.

Do not automatically open an interior window.

## 13. Deferred Preparation

`NIN9ONE_SPATIAL_EXPERIENCE` may be **defined and prepared**, but it should not become the basis for speculative interior implementation before the exterior disposition.

The ideal activation point is:

> **after exterior PASS and before authorization of Threshold + First Authored Encounter.**

This preserves sequencing:

```text
Exterior experience
        ↓
Completion gate
        ↓
First-contact evidence
        ↓
Founder disposition
        ↓
Exterior PASS
        ↓
Spatial skill activation
        ↓
Threshold + one authored encounter
        ↓
Future evidence
```

## 14. Explicit Non-Authorizations

This disposition does not authorize:

- interior implementation;
- Trophy Room canonization;
- Throne Room canonization;
- new lore;
- `WORLD_CANON.md` changes;
- additional worlds;
- platform infrastructure;
- game systems;
- progression systems;
- multiplayer;
- accounts;
- commerce;
- unrestricted asset acquisition;
- Blender adoption as a default production pipeline;
- a new autonomous implementation window beyond the three moves above.

## Founder Direction

> **Close the exterior through evidence rather than builder intuition. Preserve progressive revelation. Build no more of the Citadel than the experience has earned. Prepare the spatial discipline required for the first interior proof, but do not reveal the interior before the exterior has done its job.**
