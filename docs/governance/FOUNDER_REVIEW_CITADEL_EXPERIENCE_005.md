## 2. What I Learned

- Sonic specificity did not require more content. Very slow movement inside the existing graph was enough to keep the soundscape from resolving as a fixed generic bed while preserving restraint and player-controlled silence.
- Compilation and source inspection were insufficient for the keyboard route. Rendered first-gesture testing exposed a state-update timing failure that pointer testing did not reveal.
- The existing hidden native controls remain valuable precisely because they can preserve keyboard and assistive access without restoring a persistent discovery interface.
- Landmark response felt more environmental when its consequence extended into Black Water, the established reflective connective layer, rather than remaining isolated to the selected object.
- The Citadel's central reflection provides a strong hierarchy boundary. Side-landmark echoes can register as consequence only when they remain materially quieter and spatially smaller than that central field.
- The internal discovery-state architecture continues to support meaningful world response without becoming player-facing progression.
- Fresh use correctly moved the window across sound, accessibility, and environmental discovery rather than treating one implementation lane as a three-ticket checklist.

## 3. Current Experience
