## 2. What I Learned

- A composed arrival did not by itself establish approach. Moving through distance toward the existing composition made the exterior feel more like a place without requiring a new route or camera system.
- Interface-light did not mean leaving the primary movement grammar entirely implicit. One brief cue was enough to distinguish the Citadel from a fixed scene while avoiding a persistent HUD.
- Magnification alone did not make manual approach feel environmental. A reversible response in the existing Black Water gave proximity a quiet material consequence without resolving the entrance.
- The three changes reinforce one arc—arrival, orientation, and environmental answer—while preserving the landscape rather than expanding it.
- Technical and authored-browser evidence can establish stability and intended response, but it cannot satisfy the landscape completion gate by itself. The gate explicitly requires observation with people who were not involved in building the experience.
- Threshold legibility remains unresolved. The work improved the credibility of approach but did not identify, build, or imply the final threshold.
- Product integrity and canon integrity remained separate review questions. These moves advanced arrival and place while leaving confirmed world meaning entirely under `WORLD_CANON.md`.

## 3. Current Experience

The Citadel now opens as an approach rather than only a tableau. The visitor begins farther from the established resting composition, advances along the Black Water axis, and receives one quiet indication that the place can be explored. Manual approach beyond the resting view produces a restrained, reversible response in the existing central reflection, so movement has an environmental consequence rather than reading only as camera zoom.

The Citadel remains visually dominant within the solar field. Restrained orbit, environment-led landmark discovery, contextual reflections, side-landmark echoes, and opt-in procedural sound remain intact. The experience still avoids a persistent HUD, quest marker, visible progression, release-audio surface, explanatory lore, and conventional media-player framing.

The exterior is stronger, but it has not been declared experience-complete. People uninvolved in building it have not yet been observed against the gate, surrounding-environment distinction remains an evidence question, and the entrance or threshold has not yet been shown to be environmentally legible.

The adopted product vision remains broader than music delivery. Music may be the world's gravitational force, but the current exterior continues to stand on place, atmosphere, discovery, state, and response without requiring prior knowledge of a release. No additional fictional world has been inferred from the NIN9ONE Universe product umbrella.
