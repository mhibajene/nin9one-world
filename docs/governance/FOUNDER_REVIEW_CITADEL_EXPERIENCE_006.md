## 2. What I Learned

- Camera freedom was not automatically exploration. The earlier vertical and lateral ranges exposed more geometry but weakened the Citadel as a place by breaking its silhouette, horizon, solar field, and Black Water relationships.
- Restrained navigation still produces meaningful discovery when it reveals foreground scale, parallax, water, and side-landmark relationships without surrendering the central composition.
- The brightest visible symptom was not produced by the most obvious authored layer. Attenuating the additive gold meshes did not remove the upper-orbit flare; isolating the physically lit water surface identified the actual source.
- Material response could be repaired without inventing a new shader, texture, palette token, or world system. A small view-dependent balance between existing layers restored the established visual language.
- Responsive camera bounds cannot be calibrated in isolation. Narrowing the envelope revealed that the existing intro speed reached the new boundary early, so preserving the ten-second cue required proportional speed calibration.
- Entry, extremes, combined orbit, and the transition between them all mattered. A single attractive still would not have established that navigation remained coherent.
- Fresh exact-main use correctly moved the window from vertical navigation to material response and back to lateral navigation based on observed weaknesses rather than treating one lane as a three-ticket checklist.

## 3. Current Experience

The Citadel remains visually primary on entry, held inside the solar field and joined to the player by the Black Water approach. The initial composition, interface-light discovery surface, contextual reflections, side-landmark echoes, and opt-in sound control remain intact.

Lateral exploration now reads as parallax within one place rather than displacement across a set. Both responsive extremes preserve the Citadel, solar field, water path, and horizon as a coherent axis. The ten-second intro drift remains gradual and continuous, stops on player input, and is absent under reduced motion.

Pointer and keyboard routes still reach the established landmark reflections. Escape dismissal and focus return remain intact. Sound remains off by default and subordinate to the place.
