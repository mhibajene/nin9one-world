## 2. What I Learned

- Faceted geometry alone did not make Obsidian Matter read materially in this dark composition. Its planar character became legible only when restrained raking light supplied enough contrast, showing that material and illumination must be judged together.
- The Nexus IV archive was useful as a non-canonical evidence source because it clarified a visual tendency without requiring asset copying, lore extraction, or a canonical conclusion.
- The strength of the discovery response depends on how it arrives, not only on its target intensity. Easing the established illumination made the same state architecture feel more like environmental attention and less like an interface switch.
- The first two moves completed a coherent material-response pass. Fresh use then made silence, rather than another visual refinement, the most important remaining atmospheric absence.
- A small native audio graph was sufficient to test the Citadel's first sonic grammar. A content pipeline, playback system, music library, or new dependency was not required.
- Opt-in activation and intentional silence are part of the experience rather than browser constraints to hide. The control can preserve agency while remaining subordinate to the place.
- Founder response established a durable sonic design principle: **Sound belongs to place before it belongs to playback.** Future release-derived soundscapes may spatially reinterpret musical DNA, but that horizon does not authorize stems, track mapping, a player, or speculative audio architecture now.
- Across all three moves, the strongest result came from changing how established material and atmosphere are perceived, not from adding more objects, lore, visible systems, or world meaning.
